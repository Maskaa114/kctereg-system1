How it works

  1. 01Teacher enters and submits course grades.
  2. 02Department Head reviews and approves them.
  3. 03Registrar Head gives the final approval.
  4. 04Student views the result on their account.

Results stay hidden until step 3 is complete — every mark on your transcript has been through both approvals.
